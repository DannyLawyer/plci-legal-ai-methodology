# PLCI: A Legal AI Practice Methodology

> A documentation-first framework for traceable legal knowledge workflows, human review, and governance.

[中文主文](README.md) · [License](LICENSE)

PLCI describes a legal AI practice method rather than a legal-answer engine. It separates source materials, knowledge drafts, drafting assets, and human decisions so that each item retains a clear purpose, confidence state, and review boundary.

## What it demonstrates

- Traceability from a material claim back to a precisely scoped source segment.
- Separate states for transcription checking, citation completeness, substantive review, reuse clearance, and final approval.
- Human-in-the-loop controls for uncertainty, legal judgment, and irreversible actions.
- A distinct boundary between clause wording provenance and legal authority.
- Documentation-first governance with minimal permissions and recoverable changes.

## Repository scope

This repository contains rewritten methodology documentation only. It deliberately excludes source materials, contract language, case materials, implementation code, operational records, personal notes, and real examples.

It is not legal advice, deployable software, or a substitute for source, licensing, or jurisdiction-specific legal analysis.
